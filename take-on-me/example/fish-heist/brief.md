# The Fish Heist

Simon's Cat × a-ha "Take On Me". 25 s, 1440×1080 (4:3), silent, 100 % procedural canvas drawing
(one `<surface>`, 12 drawings per second, hard cuts only).

Two worlds, one engine:

- **Kitchen (toon):** flat colour fills + wobbly black marker line, steady, clean. No hatching, no grain on the line.
- **Sketch world (pencil):** the graphite engine from `pencil-style-test.html` — boiling strokes, hatched tone, paper tooth, gate weave.
- **Crossovers** are the joke: pencil things inside the colour world (reflection, hand, fish, fists) sit on a patch of paper;
  the only colour inside the pencil world is the warm light in the torn hole.
